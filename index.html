For us, gau seva means showing up for the lives that depend on us.

At Sri Mahagowri Lalitha Rajarajeshwari Charitable Trust, our cow shelter is home to 54 cows. Their needs are simple, but ongoing: nourishment, fresh water, a sheltered place to rest, and attention to their wellbeing.

We invite you to be part of that care. Whether you contribute to fodder, offer supplies, or help more people discover the shelter, your kindness can make a difference to their everyday lives.

A few helpful answers

Before you
lend a hand.

Have a different question?
We would love to hear from you.

How many cows are currently at the shelter?+
We currently care for 54 cows. Your support can help with their nourishment, shelter and everyday wellbeing.
How do I make a donation?+
Choose a suggested amount or enter your own in the donation section. You can then request payment details from the trust through WhatsApp or email. Please confirm the recipient and payment details directly before transferring money. This website does not take payments.
Can I contribute every month?+
Yes, you can express interest in monthly support using the donation form. The trust will help you discuss the arrangement. Selecting monthly support here does not create a subscription or an automatic payment.
Can I donate fodder or other supplies?+
Please contact the trust about current needs, suitable supplies and delivery arrangements before you purchase or send items. This helps ensure your contribution can be put to good use.
Can I receive a receipt or claim a tax benefit?+
Please ask the trust about donation receipts and any applicable tax benefits before contributing. Tax deductions are not promised on this website.
Can I contribute from outside India?+
Please speak with the trust about donation eligibility before sending funds from outside India. International online payments are not available on this website.
Where is the shelter, and can I visit?+
Please contact the trust to arrange a visit and receive the shelter’s location. The registered office is in Bengaluru Urban, Karnataka; it is not listed here as the shelter’s visiting address.
